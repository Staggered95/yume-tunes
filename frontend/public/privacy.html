YumeTunes Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

YumeTunes ("we", "our") is an anime music streaming web application available at https://yumetunes.duckdns.org. This policy explains what user data we collect, how we use it, and how you can manage or delete it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Google Sign-In Data: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name, email address, and profile picture URL using basic authentication scopes (openid, email, profile). If you register manually, we collect your username, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • App Activity: Playlists you create, songs you like, playback history, and theme preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and maintain your login session via secure HTTP-only cookies.
  • To save and synchronize your playlists, liked tracks, and listening recommendations across sessions.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or Google user data to third parties. Data is stored in our application database solely to provide YumeTunes features. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data while your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing yumetunes9@gmail.com.
